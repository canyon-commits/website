About

Canyon is a global technology investor across buyouts, growth equity, and venture. We are a team of investors, operators, and engineers with experience across Silicon Valley and Asia. We take an owner-operator approach and invest for the long term.

Invest

We invest in technology companies globally, at any stage. We provide capital, hands-on operational support, and access to our extensive network to drive value creation. We put that network behind every company we back.

Acquire & Transform

We acquire software, consumer technology, and technology-enabled businesses and hold them for the long term. We use AI to transform these companies and expand their margins, restructuring them around talent, technology, and data. Across our portfolio, this has produced revenue growth, cost reductions, and higher earnings. Our companies serve enterprise and government clients globally.

Build

We selectively partner with exceptional founders to build transformative AI companies that address meaningful market gaps. We take concentrated positions, embed deeply in the business, align ownership with contribution, and stay actively involved until each company is built to last.
